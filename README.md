## Delegates

### Professor Corrado Paganelli
Dean of Dental School, University of Brescia, Italy. Chair of IFDEA and FEHDD; Former President of CECDO and ADEE. Convivial host of the Brescia Innovations in Education Colloquia for over 20 years.

### Professor Stylianos Hatzipanagos
Fellow, Centre for Online and Distance Education, University of London. Expertise in AI in learning and teaching, assessment innovation, and online/distance education.

### Dr. Nicolas Giraudeau
Dentist, academic and public health specialist at the University Hospital and University of Montpellier, France. His work focuses on digital oral health and its potential to improve prevention, access to care and health equity, particularly for underserved and vulnerable populations. He founded and coordinates e-DENT, a pioneering teledentistry programme, and leads the "Montpellier Santé Orale" programme. As a digital oral health expert for the World Health Organization, he contributed to the development of the WHO–ITU mOralHealth initiative. Holds doctorates in dental surgery and health law.

### Dr. Peter Fritz
Certified specialist in periodontics, Fonthill, Ontario. Holds an MBA (Northwestern), dual law degrees (University of Toronto), and is pursuing a master's in blockchain, cybersecurity and AI at IMD Lausanne. Chairs the Committee on AI and Emerging Digital Technology for the Royal College of Dentists of Canada.

### Professor Kenneth Anthony Eaton
Visiting Professor, University of Portsmouth; Honorary Professor, University of Kent. Past President of EADPH; Specialist in Periodontics and Dental Public Health.

### Dharmesh Chauhan
Lead Data Analyst, Argus Media. 15+ years in Business Intelligence; expertise in data visualisation, semantic data modelling, and QuickSight Analytics.

### Professor Dieter Schonwetter
Director of Student Affairs and Academic Services, Dr. Gerald Niznick College of Dentistry. Winner of the ACFD National Teaching Award (2018). 60+ publications, 200+ professional development workshops across 12 countries.

### Dr. Aristomenis I. Syngelakis
President, Council of European Chief Dental Officers (CECDO). Former Chief Dental Officer of the Hellenic Republic (2020–2025). WHO Special Advisor.

### Professor Margaret Cox
Emerita Professor of IT in Education, King's College London. OBE (2001). International expert in technology in education; past President of NCUP; Honorary Professor, Dental Academy, University of Portsmouth.

### Dr. Stephen Deboo
Senior Clinical Lecturer in Restorative Dentistry & Undergraduate Programme Director, University of Liverpool School of Dentistry. Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (SFHEA); Deputy Lead Examiner for the UK Overseas Registration Examination.

### Dr. Chantal Burgess
London-based dental surgeon; Associate Specialist in Oral Surgery, Chelsea & Westminster NHS Trust. PGCert in Dental Education (Distinction, University of Kent). Research interest in psychomotor skills and origami in dental education.

### Dr. Ann-Marie Brennan
Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Professional Practice, Kent Business School, University of Kent. Course Director, MSc Advanced & Specialist Healthcare. Specialist in forensic biology and wildlife crime; author of five books.

### Professor Barry Quinn
Chair in Restorative Dentistry & Dental Education, University of Liverpool. Secretary General of ADEE; National Teaching Fellow (2023); King James IV Professor, Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh. 190+ refereed publications.
